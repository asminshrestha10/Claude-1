## Who we are

JuneTrial is a small automotive publication built around one idea: 4x4 and off-road content should be written by people who understand *why* a vehicle works, not just how it looks in a photo. Edit this paragraph in `/about.md` to introduce your team.

## What JuneTrial does

We publish vehicle reviews, build guides, industry news and honest product recommendations for the 4x4 and off-road community. Everything we write is meant to be read before you spend money — on a vehicle, a modification, or a piece of recovery gear.

## Our automotive / 4x4 focus

We cover wagons, utes and dedicated off-roaders, with a bias toward vehicles that are genuinely used off-road rather than styled to look like they might be.

## Engineering perspective

Where most outlets stop at "it feels planted," we try to explain the suspension geometry, drivetrain layout or recovery physics behind why something works. You don't need an engineering degree to read JuneTrial — you'll just understand more by the end of the article than when you started.
