Ford didn't just lift a Ranger and fit bigger tyres — the Raptor gets a widened track, a reinforced chassis, and Fox's Live Valve dampers, which adjust damping in real time based on terrain and driving mode.

## On road

The Raptor is more composed at speed than its size suggests, though the wide track and firm low-speed damping make it feel less relaxed than a standard Ranger around town.

## Off road

This is where the Raptor is built to live. The extra wheel travel and internal-bypass damper technology mean it soaks up high-speed corrugations and jumps in a way that would break conventional shocks. It's genuinely closer to a Baja truck than a lifted ute.

## Who it's for

Buyers who prioritise high-speed off-road composure over outright payload or towing capacity — the Raptor trades some of both for its suspension travel.
