The Jimny's modest power output is almost beside the point — its light weight, short wheelbase and solid axles give it a power-to-weight and articulation advantage that far larger, more powerful 4x4s can't match in tight technical terrain.

## On road

The short wheelbase and low power mean the Jimny feels out of its depth on the highway, with more wind noise and less stability at speed than larger 4x4s.

## Off road

This is where the Jimny earns its reputation. Its light weight means it doesn't need much power to climb, and its short overhangs let it approach and depart obstacles that would ground a longer vehicle.

## Who it's for

Buyers prioritising technical off-road ability and low running costs over highway comfort, towing capacity or outright power.
