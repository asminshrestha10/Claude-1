The Rubicon trim exists for one reason: to be the most capable rock-crawler Jeep sells without modification. Solid axles at both ends, locking differentials front and rear, and an electronically disconnecting front sway bar are all standard.

## On road

Solid axles and a body-on-frame chassis mean the Wrangler doesn't ride or handle like a modern unibody SUV — there's more body roll and a firmer ride than most rivals, which is the trade-off for its off-road hardware.

## Off road

Few factory vehicles at this price match the Rubicon's rock-crawling ability. The disconnecting sway bar alone dramatically increases wheel articulation over uneven terrain, and the 4:1 low-range gearing gives fine control at walking pace.

## Who it's for

Buyers whose priority is technical low-speed off-roading — rock crawling, deep ruts, steep technical trails — over daily on-road comfort.
