The 300 Series replaced the long-serving 200 with a smaller-displacement, twin-turbo V6 that produces more torque at lower revs — a change aimed squarely at towing and low-speed off-road driving.

## On road

The twin-turbo V6 feels noticeably more responsive than the outgoing 200 Series' V8, and the 10-speed automatic keeps the engine in its torque band. Cabin refinement is a step up, though the extra electronics mean there's more to go wrong long-term than older Cruisers.

## Off road

Kinetic Dynamic Suspension System (KDSS) — an electronically disconnecting anti-roll bar setup — lets the 300 Series retain wheel articulation over rough terrain while staying composed on-road. Multi-Terrain Select and a locking centre and rear differential round out a genuinely serious off-road package.

## Who it's for

Buyers who want the LandCruiser's reputation for long-term durability with meaningfully better fuel economy and low-down torque than the older V8.
