The X-Terrain sits at the top of the D-Max range, adding equipment and styling over the workhorse variants while keeping the same drivetrain underneath.

## On road

The D-Max's ride and refinement have improved significantly over the previous generation, and the driver assistance suite is more comprehensive than most rivals offer at this price point.

## Off road

A conventional part-time 4WD system with low range and a rear locker gives the X-Terrain solid, unremarkable off-road capability — enough for most touring and light off-road work without any standout hardware.

## Who it's for

Buyers who want a well-equipped, well-warrantied dual-cab ute without paying a premium for a factory performance variant.
