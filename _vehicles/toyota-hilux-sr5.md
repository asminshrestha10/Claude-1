The HiLux isn't the most powerful or the most comfortable ute in its class, but its reputation for surviving high mileage with minimal drama is why it remains a default choice for remote-area work and touring.

## On road

The SR5's ride is firmer than car-based SUVs but composed for a leaf-sprung ute, and the 2.8L diesel's mid-range torque makes it easy to drive without constantly working the gearbox.

## Off road

A part-time 4WD system with low range and a rear locker covers most real-world off-road scenarios. It won't out-articulate a solid-axle Wrangler, but its simplicity is part of the appeal for remote touring, where fewer electronic systems means fewer things to fail a long way from help.

## Who it's for

Buyers who want proven long-term reliability and strong resale value over class-leading power or off-road articulation.
