The Patrol Y62 is the last of a dying breed: a full-size wagon with a big naturally aspirated V8 and a genuine part-time 4WD system, sold at a time when most competitors have moved to smaller turbo-diesels and permanent all-wheel drive.

## On road

The V8 gives the Patrol a smoothness diesel-powered rivals can't match, at the cost of fuel economy. Around town it drives more like a large luxury SUV than a working 4x4, helped by Nissan's Hydraulic Body Motion Control system, which uses linked hydraulic dampers instead of a conventional anti-roll bar setup.

## Off road

Low range, a locking rear differential, and generous approach and departure angles make the Patrol genuinely capable once you leave the pavement — the V8's low-down torque is more useful here than the outright power figure suggests. The solid rear axle also means better articulation over uneven terrain than most independent-rear-suspension wagons in this class.

## Who it's for

Buyers who tow, who value long-term running smoothness over outright efficiency, and who aren't in a rush to see a turbo-diesel replace a naturally aspirated engine.
