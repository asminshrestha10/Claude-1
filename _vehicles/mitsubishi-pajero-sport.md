Built on the same ladder-frame platform as the Triton ute, the Pajero Sport adds a wagon body and coil-sprung rear suspension, aiming at buyers who want ute-level off-road ability with SUV practicality.

## On road

Coil springs at the rear give the Pajero Sport a noticeably more settled ride than most leaf-sprung ute-based wagons, though it's still firmer than a unibody SUV.

## Off road

Mitsubishi's Super Select 4WD-II system allows full-time 4WD on sealed roads as well as a locked low-range mode, giving more flexibility than a typical part-time system — genuinely useful for drivers who move between highway and unsealed roads often.

## Who it's for

Families who want genuine low-range 4WD capability and seven-seat practicality without stepping up to a full-size wagon.
