The modern Defender abandoned the old model's ladder-frame-and-leaf-spring formula for a stiff unibody platform and adjustable air suspension — a significant departure that divided long-time Defender owners.

## On road

This is by far the most refined Defender Land Rover has built, with air suspension smoothing out most surfaces and a cabin that competes with premium SUVs rather than working vehicles.

## Off road

Terrain Response 2 automatically adjusts throttle mapping, differential behaviour and suspension height for the surface underneath, and the raised air suspension setting delivers genuinely competitive ground clearance and wading depth for a factory vehicle.

## Who it's for

Buyers who want Defender-level off-road capability without sacrificing the on-road refinement expected of a modern premium SUV — at a price premium over more utilitarian rivals.
