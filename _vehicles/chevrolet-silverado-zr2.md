The Silverado ZR2 brings full-size American pickup proportions to the same off-road-focused segment as the Ranger Raptor, with Multimatic spool-valve dampers — technology originally developed for motorsport — tuned for high-speed off-road driving.

## On road

The sheer size and naturally aspirated V8 give the Silverado a very different character to smaller turbocharged rivals — less agile, but with strong low-end torque and a distinctive V8 soundtrack.

## Off road

Front and rear locking differentials combined with the Multimatic dampers make the ZR2 genuinely capable at speed over rough terrain, though its width and length limit where it can be driven on tighter tracks compared with mid-size rivals.

## Who it's for

Buyers who want full-size American pickup capability and presence with a factory-tuned off-road suspension package.
