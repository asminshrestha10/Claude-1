Toyota has confirmed that a mild-hybrid variant of the next-generation HiLux will arrive in 2027, pairing the current 2.8L turbo-diesel four-cylinder with a 48-volt electric motor and battery system similar to the setup already used in the LandCruiser Prado.

## What's changing

The hybrid system is expected to primarily target fuel efficiency and low-speed torque delivery, rather than significantly increasing peak power. Toyota has not confirmed whether the hybrid variant will be offered across the full HiLux range or limited to higher trim levels initially.

## Why it matters for buyers

Emissions regulations tightening across several key ute markets are pushing manufacturers toward electrification even in traditionally diesel-dominated segments like one-tonne utes. A mild-hybrid HiLux would put Toyota roughly in step with rivals already offering hybrid assistance in this class.

## What we don't know yet

Toyota has not released pricing, towing capacity figures, or confirmation of whether the hybrid battery pack will affect payload capacity — a key consideration for tradespeople and tourers who rely on the HiLux's cargo capacity.
