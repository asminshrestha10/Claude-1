Jeep has confirmed that prototype testing of a fully electric Wrangler variant is underway, with the stated goal of retaining the model's solid-axle, low-range off-road hardware rather than moving to a unibody electric platform.

## Engineering challenges

Packaging large battery packs while preserving ground clearance and wading depth is a significant engineering challenge for a vehicle whose off-road reputation depends heavily on those figures. Jeep has not confirmed how battery placement will affect the Wrangler's approach and departure angles.

## Why Jeep is pursuing this path

Retaining solid axles and low range on an electric platform would differentiate an electric Wrangler from unibody electric SUVs, preserving the model's core off-road identity for a market of buyers who specifically value that hardware.

## Timeline

No confirmed release date has been announced. JuneTrial will update this article as further details of the testing program are released.
