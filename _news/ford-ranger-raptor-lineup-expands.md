Ford Australia has confirmed it will expand the local Ranger Raptor lineup beyond its current single-specification offering, though full details of the additional variants have not yet been released.

## What's likely to change

Industry speculation points toward a lower-priced Raptor variant with reduced equipment levels, aimed at buyers who want the Raptor's suspension and drivetrain hardware without the full equipment list of the current flagship model.

## Why this matters

The current Ranger Raptor sits at a significant price premium over the standard Ranger range. A lower-cost variant would broaden the Raptor's appeal to buyers currently priced out of factory-built off-road performance utes.

## What we're watching for

JuneTrial will update this article once Ford confirms specific trim details, pricing and availability dates for the expanded lineup.
