Industry analysts are forecasting fuel price movement heading into 2027 that could affect the running cost of long-distance touring trips, particularly for larger-displacement petrol vehicles and diesel utes covering high remote-area distances.

## Why this matters for tourers

Fuel typically represents one of the largest variable costs on an extended touring trip, and price movement compounds over the long distances covered on trips like a Simpson Desert or Cape York crossing.

## What tourers can do

Trip planning that accounts for a fuel price buffer, along with fuel-efficient driving techniques (correct tyre pressure, sensible loading, avoiding unnecessary idling) can meaningfully offset rising costs without changing the vehicle you drive.

## JuneTrial's take

We'll continue tracking how fuel price movement affects our own trip cost estimates and will factor updated figures into future trip reports.
