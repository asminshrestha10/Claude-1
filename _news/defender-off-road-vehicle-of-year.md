The Land Rover Defender 110 has been named this year's Off-Road Vehicle of the Year, with judges citing the combination of genuine off-road hardware and on-road refinement as the deciding factor over more purpose-built rivals.

## What the judges highlighted

Judging criteria reportedly weighted off-road capability, on-road comfort, and overall value relatively evenly — a combination the Defender's air suspension and Terrain Response 2 system were noted as handling well compared with more utilitarian competitors.

## How this compares to previous years

The Defender's win continues a trend of unibody, air-suspension-equipped off-roaders performing well in awards that weight on-road refinement alongside off-road capability, a shift from years when body-on-frame vehicles with solid axles dominated these categories.
