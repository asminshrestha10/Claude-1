Isuzu has issued a voluntary recall covering a range of D-Max models built within a specific production window, citing a potential fault with a steering column component that could, in rare cases, affect steering control.

## Which vehicles are affected

The recall applies to specific D-Max models built during an identified production period. Isuzu has stated affected owners will be contacted directly and that the repair will be carried out free of charge.

## What owners should do

Owners unsure whether their vehicle is affected should contact their nearest Isuzu dealer with their vehicle identification number (VIN) to confirm, rather than waiting for a recall notice to arrive by mail.

## JuneTrial's take

Recalls like this are a normal part of vehicle ownership and reflect manufacturers catching issues before they become widespread — but affected owners should get the repair scheduled promptly rather than delaying.
