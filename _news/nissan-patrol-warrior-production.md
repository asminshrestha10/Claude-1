Nissan has confirmed the Patrol Warrior — a locally engineered variant developed in partnership with an aftermarket suspension specialist — has entered production following an extended local testing and tuning program.

## What sets the Warrior apart

The Warrior variant is expected to include a retuned suspension package, upgraded off-road tyres, and additional underbody protection over the standard Patrol Y62, aimed at buyers who want factory-backed off-road upgrades rather than sourcing them aftermarket.

## Why local engineering matters

Locally developed variants like the Warrior are tuned specifically for the conditions they'll be driven in, which can result in a more well-sorted product for local buyers than a suspension package designed primarily for other markets.
