Updated guidelines affecting common 4x4 modifications — including suspension lift height limits and the certification process for GVM (Gross Vehicle Mass) upgrades — have been announced, with a transition period before full enforcement begins.

## What's changing

The revised rules are aimed at standardising the engineering certification process across states, following inconsistencies that previously meant a modification legally certified in one state wasn't automatically recognised in another.

## What this means for owners

Owners planning a suspension lift or GVM upgrade should confirm with a licensed vehicle engineer that any planned modification will meet the updated requirements before proceeding, rather than assuming existing certifications will carry over unchanged.

## JuneTrial's take

Clearer national standards are broadly positive for buyers, even if it means some previously common modifications need re-certification. We'll publish a full breakdown once final documentation is released.
